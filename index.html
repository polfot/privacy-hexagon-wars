Privacy and terms

Hexagon Wars · last updated 26 October 2026

In short

Hexagon Wars keeps as little about you as it can: a username you choose, your game progress and your results. No e-mail, no real name, no location, no payment details. Nothing to buy, and no ads.

What we keep, if you make an account

  • Your username (3–16 letters, digits or _).
  • Your login code: only a scrambled form of it (a hash), never the code itself. The code stays on your devices: anyone who has it can open your account, so keep it to yourself.
  • Your progress: coins, cash, level, unlocked maps, chosen difficulty.
  • Your results: daily missions, and the levels, countries, battles and multiplayer games you win online - when they were played, how long they took and the points they gave - and your weekly ranking prizes.

Without an account, nothing about you is kept on our server: the game keeps your progress only in your browser, on your device.

Why

Only to run the game: to let you play the same account on several devices, and to make the weekly ranking.

What other players see

Your username and your points this week (in the weekly ranking), and your username in multiplayer games.

Where it is kept

The game runs on Render (servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and its database is at Turso (servers in Ireland), both in the European Union. We use no other services and share nothing with anyone else.

How long, and deleting it

As long as your account exists. In the game, Account → Delete account removes your account, your progress and all your results from our server at once and for good. A game that was started and never finished is removed after a day anyway.

Can't open the game? Write to stradaminigames@gmail.com with your username and the 15-digit login code (so that we know the account is yours), and we'll delete it, with all its data, within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask what we keep about you, have it corrected or deleted (you can delete it yourself, see above). Write to us at stradaminigames@gmail.com.

Children

If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you make an account.

Playing fair

  • Some opponents may be computer-controlled.
  • Cheating, using several accounts to win prizes, or offensive usernames may get an account's results or the account removed.
  • Coins, cash, points and prizes are part of the game only: they have no money value and can't be exchanged for anything outside it.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
